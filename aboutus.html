Our Story

SwaDesi Bazaar was born from a simple idea: to create a platform where local businesses can thrive without the burden of excessive fees and taxes. We believe in the power of community and the importance of supporting local economies.

Our platform connects buyers directly with small business owners, farmers, artisans, salons, parlors, and local markets in their vicinity. Using GPS technology, we help you discover the hidden gems in your neighborhood.

Unlike other platforms, SwaDesi Bazaar is completely free for both sellers and buyers. We don't charge any commission, and there's no GST involved. Every rupee you spend goes directly to the business owner.

Why Choose SwaDesi Bazaar

Zero Commission

We don't charge any commission or fees. Sellers keep 100% of their earnings, and buyers pay exactly what the product costs.

GPS Based Discovery

Find local businesses, farmers markets, and artisans near you with our intelligent location-based search.

Support Local Economy

By shopping through SwaDesi Bazaar, you're directly contributing to your local community and economy.
